The number of clients is a key setting in the FL scenarios.       which is a feature-level attack.
To verify the applicability of our proposed method, we eval-
uate the impact on the performance of BD P FL by varying
the number of selected clients in each round. Specifically,
we vary the proportion of clients selected for aggregation in
each round by 10%, 20%, 40%, and 80%. Our experiment
was evaluated for DBA on three datasets. In general, BD P FL
is effective in different client number settings. Although we
can see from Table 4 that the success rate of backdoor attacks
decreases with the increase in the number of clients, we think
it is mainly because more clients decrease the aggregation
weights of attackers. In addition, more number of participants
in the aggregation has a slight improvement in the accuracy
of the FL global model. This indicates that the mutual dis-
tillation strategy adopted by BD P FL can effectively maintain
the personalization of the local model while ensuring that the
